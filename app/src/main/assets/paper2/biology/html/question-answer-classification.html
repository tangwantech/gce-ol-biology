Classification

1. Define the following terms as used in classification: Systematics, Binomial nomenclature, Species

Systematics is the grouping of organisms on the basis of structural similarities and differences.

Binomial nomenclature is the system of giving an organism two unique latin names which together forms the scientific name. The first name is called the genus while the second is the species name. For example man is known as Homo sapiens

Species is a group of organisms that are very similar and can interbreed to produce fertile offspring.

2. Why do we classify livings?

  • To create a classification scheme so that newly discovered organisms can be conviniently fitted into the scheme without upsetting the order
  • To make studies easier
  • To ease communication between scientist
  • To create order out of disorder
  • To show evolutionary relationship between different groups of organism

3. List the seven taxa of grouping living organism, starting from the broadest to the most specific?

Kingdom, Phylum, Class, Order, Family, Genus and Species.
