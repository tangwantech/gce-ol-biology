Nutrition in Animals

Nutrition in animals is holozoic, involving the ingestion of complex organic substances. This process requires specialized systems for digestion and absorption.

Key Processes

  • Ingestion: Taking in food through the mouth.
  • Digestion: Breaking down food into smaller, soluble molecules.
  • Absorption: Taking up digested food into the bloodstream.
  • Assimilation: Using absorbed nutrients for energy and growth.
  • Egestion: Eliminating undigested waste.

Important Terms

Click any term to get its definition:

  • Enzymes: Biological catalysts that speed up chemical reactions.
  • Dentition: The arrangement and condition of teeth in a particular species.
  • Peristalsis: Wave-like muscle contractions that move food through the digestive tract.
  • Villus: Finger-like projections in the small intestine that increase surface area for absorption.

Proper nutrition is essential for maintaining health and providing the energy needed for all life processes.
