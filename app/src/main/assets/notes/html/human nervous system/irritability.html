Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Define irritability
  • Define a stimulus and give examples
  • Define receptors and give examples of different types and the stimuli they detect

Irritability

Irritability is the ability of living organisms to respond to stimuli. The nervous system plays a crucial role in mediating these responses, allowing organisms to detect and react to changes in their environment.

Stimulus

A stimulus is any change in the internal or external environment that will bring about a response from an organisms. Examples of stimuli include: Light, Sound, Temperature, Chemicals, Touch, Pressure, etc

Receptors

Receptors are specialized cells or structures that detect stimuli and convert them into electrical signals that can be processed by the nervous system.

Different types of receptors are sensitive to specific types of stimuli. For example, photoreceptors (rods and cones) in the eyes detect light, mechanoreceptors (meissner's corpuscles and pacinian corpuscles) in the skin detect touch and pressure, and chemoreceptors (olfactory receptors and taste buds) in the nose and tongue detect chemical stimuli such as odors and tastes respectively.
