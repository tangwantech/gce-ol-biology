Neurons

Neurons are the basic structural and functional units of the nervous system. They transmit electrical signals, allowing them to communicate with each other and with other cells in the body.

Types of Neurons

There are three main types of neurons:

  • Sensory neurons: Their cell body is located along their axis. Their dendron is longer than axon. They transmit signals (nerve impulses) from sensory receptors to the central nervous system.
  • Motor neurons: Their cell body is located in the central nervous system. Their axon is longer than dendrons. They transmit signals from the central nervous system to muscles or glands.
  • Interneurons (Relay Neurons): Their cell body is located in the central nervous system. They transmit signals from sensory to motor neurons.

Nerves

Nerves are bundles of axons that carry electrical signals between the central nervous system and the rest of the body.

Individual nerve cells in a nerve are bound together by connective tissue called neurilemma or neuroglia. Another connective tissue called epineurium wraps around the entire nerve, providing protection and support.

Types of nerves

Nerves can be classified into three types based on their function:

  • Sensory nerves: These are composed of sensory neurons only that carry signals from sensory receptors to the central nervous system.
  • Motor nerves: These are composed of motor neurons only that carry signals from the central nervous system to muscles or glands.
  • Mixed nerves: These contain both sensory and motor fibers, allowing them to transmit signals to and from the central nervous system.

Summary

Neurons are the basic functional units of the nervous system. They help to carry information rapidly from one part of the body to another. Sensory neurons carry information from receptors to the central nervous system (CNS), motor neurons carry information from the CNS to muscles or glands, and interneurons connect neurons within the CNS. Nerves are bundles of axons wrapped in connective tissue and can be sensory, motor, or mixed.
