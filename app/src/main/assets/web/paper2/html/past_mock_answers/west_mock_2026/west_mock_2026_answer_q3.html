West Mock 2026 - Answer Q3

  1. Define the following terms as used in ecology

    1. Interdependence (3 marks)

      • Interdependence refers to the mutual reliance of living organisms on each other for survival e.g. flowering plants rely on insects for pollination

    2. Eutrophication (4 marks)

      • Eutrophication refers to the excessive growth of algae in water bodies due to high nutrient levels. When the algae die, they are decomposed, leading to oxygen depletion, suffocation and death of aquatic life e.g. fish

    3. Trophic level (2 marks)

      • Trophic level refers to the position of an organism in a food chain. e.g green plants (producers) occupy the first trophic level, herbivores the second, and carnivores the third

  2. Distinguish between food chain and food web. (6 marks)

    Food Chain Food Web
    Linear arrangement of organisms Complex network of interconnected food chains
    Each organism has only one food source Each organism has multiple food sources
    Involves fewer organisms Involves many organisms
    Flow of energy is linear Flow of energy is complex
    Death of one organism disrupts the food chain Death of one organism does not disrupt the food web
    Only one organism at each trophic level More than one organim at each trophic level

  3. How does thunderstorm affect the circulation of nitrogen in nature? (5 marks)

    • During thunderstorms, lightning provides the energy needed to split nitrogen gas into free radicals. These radicals are highly reactive and combine with oxygen (O2) to form nitrogen dioxide (NO2)
    • Nitrogen dioxide dissolves in rainwater to form nitric acid (HNO3)
    • The nitric acid fall to the ground in rain drops and seep into the soil where it reacts with minerals to form nitrates.
    • Plants absorb the nitrates from the soil and use them to synthesize proteins and nucleic acids
    • This process helps to replenish the nitrogen content in the soil, promoting plant growth and maintaining the nitrogen cycle
