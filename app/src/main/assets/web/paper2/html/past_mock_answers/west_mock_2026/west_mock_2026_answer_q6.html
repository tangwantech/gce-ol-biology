West Mock 2026 - Answer Q6

  1. What do you understand by the following terms:

    1. Genetic engineering (3 marks)

      Genetic engineering is the direct or deliberate modification of the genetic material (DNA) of an organism by inserting, deleting, or replacing specific genes to produce desired traits (characteristics).

    2. Biotechnology (3 marks)

      Biotechnology is the use of living organisms, cells or biological processes to produce useful products or improve organisms for human benefit.

    3. Plamid (3 marks)

      These are small, circular DNA molecules found in bacteria, separate from the chromosomal DNA and can replicate independently within bacterial cells. They are often used as vectors to transfer genes in genetic engineering.

  2. How is genetic engineering useful to man? (6 marks)

    Genetic engineering is used by man for the following:

    • Production of human insulin
    • Production of industrial enzymes
    • Production of disease resistant plants and animals
    • Treatment of genetic disorders through gene therapy
    • Improvement of food nutritional value e.g. vitamin C rich tomatoes
    • Improvement of crop yield and quality e.g. pest resistant crops

  3. Explain to your community how beer can be easily produced. (5 marks)

    Production of beer is as follows:
    1. Grains e.g. barley are soaked in water and allowed to germinate. Amylases in the germinating seed convert starch to sugars.
    2. The germinated grains are dried, crushed, and then mixed with warm water.
    3. The mixture is boiled and filtered to obtain a surgary liquid called wort.
    4. Yeast is added to the wort and fermentation occurs, producing alcohol and carbon dioxide.
    5. The fermented liquid (beer) is allowed to mature, then filtered and packaged for consumption.
