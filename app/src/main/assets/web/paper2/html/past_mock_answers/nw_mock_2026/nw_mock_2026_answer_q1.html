NorthWest Mock 2026 - Answer Q1

  1. What characteristic features do birds and mammals have in common?

    (4 marks)
    • Both are warm-blooded (endothermic)
    • Both have a pair of eyes.
    • Fertilization is internal.
    • Both are are heterotrophic.
    • Both have a four-chambered heart.
    • Both have a diaphragm for breathing.
    • Both carry out parental care of their young.
    • Both have a well-developed nervous system.
    • Both have a closed circulatory system.
    • Both have a backbone (vertebrates).

  2. State the functions of:

    1. Vagina (2 marks)
      • Serves as site for deposition of sperm during sexual intercourse
      • Serves as birth canal
      • Allows passage of menstrual flow
    2. Breast (1 marks)
      • Produces milk for nursing offspring
    3. Testis in mammals (2 marks)
      • Produces sperm
      • Produces testosterone for development of male characteristics

  3. How is the Tilapia fish adapted to life in water? (6 marks)

    • They have eyes for vision, enabling them to locate food as well as predators
    • They have gills for gaseous exchange in water
    • They have scales and mucus to prevent mechanical injury and reduce friction during swimming
    • They have lateral line to detect vibrations and pressure changes in water, enabling the fish to locate prey or incoming predator
    • They have pelvic and pectoral fins to steer the fish, acts as brakes and control pitching
    • They have caudal or tail fin to propel the fish forward during swimming
    • They have a streamlined body to reduce water resistance during swimming.
    • They have powerful trunk muscles to generate force which propels the fish during swimming
    • Some have a swim bladder to provide buoyancy. This saves energy by avoiding the need to keep swimming in order to stay at the same depth.

  4. Describe the economic importance of insects. (5 marks)

      Useful effects:

    • Insects such as bees and butterfly help in pollination of flowering plants
    • Insects serve as food to some animals e.g termites
    • Insects can be used in scientific research e.g. aphids, mosquitoes
    • Insects such as silkworms are used in the production of silk
    • Insects such as honeybees produce honey which is used as food and medicine
    • Insects such as ladybugs are used in biological control of pests
    • Burrows of some insects e.g. termites help in drainage and aeration of soil

      • Harmful effects:

    • Some insects e.g. locusts, grasshoppers, aphids, etc can cause damage to crops
    • Insects such as mosquitoes and houseflies are vectors for diseases like malaria and typhoid respectively
    • Insects such as termites cause damage to wooden structures
