Miscellaneous 2026 - Question 4

  1. Differentiate between mitosis and meiosis

    Mitosis Meiosis
    Involves one division Involves two divisions
    Produces two daughter cells Produces four daughter cells
    Daughter cells are genetically identical to the parent cell and to each other Daughter cells are genetically different from the parent cell and from each other
    Occurs in somatic (body) cells Occurs in germ cells (cells that give rise to gametes)
    Involved in growth, repair and asexual reproduction Involved in sexual reproduction and genetic diversity
    Chromosome number is maintained (diploid to diploid) Chromosome number is halved (diploid to haploid)
    No crossing over of genetic material occurs Crossing over of genetic material occurs during prophase I, leading to genetic variation

  2. State the importance of mitosis and meiosis in multicellular organisms?

    Importance of mitosis:

    • it forms the basis of growth and development
    • it enables tissue repair and regeneration
    • it facilitates asexual reproduction (production of new offspring without fusion of gametes)

    Importance of meiosis:

    • it produces gametes (sperm and egg cells) necessary for sexual reproduction
    • it promotes genetic diversity through independent assortment and crossing over, which is important for evolution and adaptation
    • it prevents doubling of chromosome number in offspring produced through fertilization.

  3. State the sites of mitosis and meiosis in plants and animals

    Sites of mitosis in plants and animals:

    • In plants: Meristematic tissues (e.g., apical meristems at root and shoot tips)
    • In animals: Bone marrow, skin, lining of digestive tract

    Sites of meiosis in plants and animals:

    • In plants: Anthers (for pollen production) and ovaries (for ovule production)
    • In animals: Ovaries (for egg production) and testes (for sperm production)
