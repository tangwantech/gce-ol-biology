West Mock 2026 - Answer Q4

  1. Define the following terms:

    1. Excretion (3 marks)

      • Excretion is the process of removing metabolic waste products from the body or substances in excess of the body's immediate needs, which if allowed to accumulate, can be harmful. e.g removal of urea and carbon dioxide

    2. Fertilization (3 marks)

      • Fertilization is the fusion of haploid male and female gametes to form a diploid zygote.

    3. Copulation (3 marks)

      • Copulation is the act of mating, which involves the forward and backward movement of erect penis inside a receptive vagina, eventually leading to ejaculation and deposition of sperm into the female reproductive tract for fertilization.

  2. Differentiate between haploid and diploid cells and give two examples of each. (6 marks)

    Haploid cell Diploid cell
    Contains only one set of chromosomes (n) Contains two sets of chromosomes (2n)
    Formed by meiosis Formed by mitosis or fertilization
    Involved in sexual reproduction Involved in growth, repair and asexual reproduction
    Examples: sperm cell, egg cell Examples: muscle cell, nerve cell
    Genetically different from the parent cell Genetically identical to the parent cell

  3. Describe the process occurring from the time the pollen grain reaches the stigma to seed formation in flowering plant. (5 marks)

    1. When a pollen grain falls on a mature stigma, it absorbs a sugar fluid, swells and germinates into a pollen tube
    2. The nucleus of the pollen grain divides into two nuclei namely tube nucleus and generative nucleus
    3. The tube nuvleus is found close to the tip of the pollen tube while the generative nucleus follows behind.
    4. As the pollen tube grows through the style towards the ovule in the ovary, it is nourished by the tissies of the style
    5. The generative nucleus soon divides by mitosis into two haploid nuclei (male gametes)
    6. When the pollen tube reaches the micropyle of the ovule, its tip breaks open, the tube nucleus degenerates, and the two male gametes are released into the embryo sac in the ovule.
    7. One of the male gametes fertilizes the egg cell to form a diploid zygote, while the other male gamete fuses with two polar nuclei to form the triploid primary endosperm. This process is called double fertilization.
    8. The zygote soon undergoes repeated mitotic divisions to form the embryo, which consists of a radicle, plumule, and cotyledons, while the primary endosperm develops into the nutritive endosperm.
    9. After fertilization, the ovule develops into a seed, and the ovary develops into a fruit.
    10. The petals, sepals, style and stigma wither and fall off.
