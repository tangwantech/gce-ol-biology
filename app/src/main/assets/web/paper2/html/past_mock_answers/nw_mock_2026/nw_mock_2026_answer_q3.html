NorthWest Mock 2026 - Answer Q3

  1. What do you understand by the following terms as used in ecology:

    1. Ecosystem (5 marks)

      An ecosystem is a natural unit, consisting of all the living organisms (biotic components e.g. plants, animals, microorganisms) and their physical environment (abiotic or non-living components e.g. soil, water, air) interacting with each other to produce a stable and self-sustaining system. e.g. tropical rainforest

    2. Conservation (4 marks)

      Conservation refers to the protection, preservation, and sustainable management of natural resources and biodiversity in order to prevent the decline or extinction of species.

  2. Differentiate between a food chain and a food web. (6 marks)

    Food Chain Food Web
    Linear arrangement of organisms Complex network of interconnected food chains
    Each organism has only one food source Each organism has multiple food sources
    Involves fewer organisms Involves many organisms
    Flow of energy is linear Flow of energy is complex
    Death of one organism disrupts the food chain Death of one organism does not disrupt the food web
    Only one organism at each trophic level More than one organim at each trophic level

  3. How may the extinction of species of organisms in an ecosystem be reduced? (5 marks)

    • Creation of zoos, parks or botanical gardens to maintain species habitat.
    • By breeding and reintroducing endangered species into the wild.
    • By reducing pollution through sustainable practices.
    • By enforcing laws and regulations that protect endangered species and their habitats.
    • By raising awareness and educating the public about the importance of biodiversity and conservation efforts.
    • Creation of seed and sperm banks.
