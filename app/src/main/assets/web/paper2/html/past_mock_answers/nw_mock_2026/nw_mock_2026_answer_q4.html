NorthWest Mock 2026 - Answer Q4

    1. What is photosynthesis? (5 marks)

      Photosynthesis is the process by which all green plants, some protoctists and bacteria, manufacture carbohydrate e.g. glucose, by combining carbon dioxide and water, using sunlight energy, absorbed by chlorophyll, with the release of oxygen gas as a waste product.

    2. State the importance of photosynthesis to living organisms (5 marks)

      • Photosynthesis produces carbohydrate, protein and fats which serves as food to animals and other heterotrophs.
      • Photosynthesis produces oxygen used by aerobic organisms for respiration
      • Photosynthesis removes excess carbon dioxide from the atmosphere, thereby reducing the effects of global warming and climate change.
      • Glucose produced during photosynthesis is broken down during respiration to produce energy
      • Glucose produced during photosynthesis can be converted to cellulose for the formation of plant cell wall
      • Petrol and oil used in automobils are products of plant degradation millions of years ago

  2. How is photosynthesis different from respiration? (6 marks)

    Photosynthesis Respiration
    Occurs in chloroplasts Occurs in mitochondria
    Uses sunlight energy Does not use sunlight energy
    Produces glucose and oxygen Uses glucose and oxygen
    Uses carbon dioxide and water Produces carbon dioxide and water
    Uses energy (endothermic) Releases energy (exothermic)
    Anabolic process Catabolic process
    Leads to increase in weight Leads to decrease in weight
    Occurs only in cells with chloroplast or chlorophyll Occurs in all living cells

  3. Using the Biuret reagent, describe experimentally how you wound test for the presence of protein in a food sample. (5 marks)

    Aim: To test for the presence of protein in a food sample e.g. beans
    Requirements: Dilute NaOH, 1% CuSO₄ solution, test tube and syringe
    Procedure:.
    1. Crush the food sample e.g. boiled beans in a bowl.
    2. Add water to the bowl and mix.
    3. Filter the mixture to obtain a clear solution.
    4. Add 2 cm³ of the clear solution to a test tube.
    5. Add 1 cm³ of NaOH to the test tube.
    6. Add few drops of 1% CuSO₄ solution to the test tube.
    7. Shake the test tube to mix the contents.
    Observation:

    A violet or purple colour indicates the presence of protein in the food sample.
