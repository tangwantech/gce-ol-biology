  2. Describe the role of blood in defense and transport in mammals. (6 marks)

    Function of blood in transport:

    • Transports oxygen form lungs to tissues for respiration
    • Transports carbon dioxide from tissues to lungs for excretion
    • Transports hormones from endocrine glands to target organs
    • Transports end products of digestion from the intestines to the liver and other parts of the body
    • Transports nitrogenous wastes e.g urea to the kidneys for excretion
    • Transports heat throughout the body to help regulate body temperature
    • Transports drugs from points of administration to infected areas

    Function of blood in defense:

    • Blood contains white blood cells e.g. phagocytes that engulf and destroy pathogens.
    • White blood cells called lymphocytes produce antibodies that help neutralize and destroy specific pathogens
    • Blood clot formation prevents entry of pathogens and excessive bleeding when blood vessels are damaged

  3. State the differences between blood and lymph. (5 marks)

    Blood Lymph
    Red in colour. White in colour
    Blood contains red blood cells Lacks red blood cells.
    Contains platelets Lacks platelets.
    Contains less metabolic wastes Contains more metabolic wastes
    Contains less lymphocytes Contains more lymphocytes
    Flows to and away from the heart Flows from tissues to the heart
    Flows rapidly Flows slowly
    Flows due to the pumping action of the heart Flows due to the contraction of skeletal muscles and the movement of the thoracic cavity
