NorthWest Mock 2026 - Answer Q2

  1. Define the following

    1. Excretion

      (3 marks)
      • Excretion is the process of removing metabolic waste products from the body or substances in excess of the body's immediate needs, which if allowed to accumulate, can be harmful. e.g removal of urea and carbon dioxide

    2. Homeostasis

      (2 marks)
      • Homeostasis is the maintenance of a relatively constant internal environment of blood or body fluids so as to allow for efficient functioning of cellular processes.

  2. What are the adaptations of the mammalian skin in the protection of the body? (4 marks)

    • The cornified layer is tough to prevent mechanical injury to underlying tissues and as well as to preventthe entry of pathogens
    • The malpighinan layer produces melanin which screens off harmful ultraviolet radiation
    • The sebaceous glands produce sebum which is anticeptic, preventing the growth of bacteria
    • Derivatives of the skin, such as nails and horns, can be used for defense.
    • Sweat glands in the skin secrete sweat which helps to prevent overheating of the body.
    • The skin is covered with hair, which provides insulation and protection against mechanical injury.

  3. Describe the role of the kidney in the regulation of water content of the body. (6 marks)

    • The kidney works together with the hypothalamus and posterior pituitary gland to regulate water balance in the body.
    • When the osmotic pressure of blood is high, such as after sweating alot or when much salt is consumed, osmoreceptors in the hypothalamus detect this change and stimulate the posterior pituitory gland to secrete antidiuretic hormone (ADH) into the bloodstream.
    • ADH is carried by blood to the kidneys, where it stimulates the distal convuluted tubules and collecting ducts to become more permeable to water.
    • Much water is reabsorbed from the glomerular filtrate back into the blood.
    • The volume of urine produced is small and very concentrated (hypertonic).
    • However, when the osmotic pressure of blood falls such as after drinking much water, the hypothalamus does not stimulate the posterior pituitary gland.
    • Little or no ADH is secreted and the distal convoluted tubules and collecting ducts become less permeable to water.
    • The volume of urine produced is much and very dilute (hypotonic).

  4. Imagine that insulin and glucagon are like two players in a team that work together to keep blood sugar level just right. Which of these two players do you think is the most important? Explain your answer saying what would happen if that player was not there. (5 marks)

    Insulin: The role of insulin is to lower blood glucose level by stimulating the liver cells to take up excess glucose and convert it to glycogen. In the absence of insulin, blood glucose level rises, a condition called hyperglycemia. This leads to a disease called diabetes, symptoms of which include: frequent urination, obesity (excessive weight gain) and even comma.
